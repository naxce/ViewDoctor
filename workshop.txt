description=[b]The minigames[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*] Stitching: pull the thread through each dot across the wound. Stray off the line or rush it and the thread tears.
description=[*] Removing stitches: snip each knot right when the closing ring meets it.
description=[*] Bandages and splints: wrap each pass across the limb at a steady pace. Taking one off is the same, the other way round.
description=[*] Disinfecting, cleaning burns, poultices: rub away the dirt before it creeps back, slowly over the open wound.
description=[*] Glass: grab each shard and pull it out slowly along its line.
description=[*] Bullets: hold the probe still until you find the bullet, then pull.
description=[*] Injections and blood draws: hit the vein, hold still, then slide slowly to push the plunger.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[b]How it plays[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*] In first person the wound, stitches, bandage wraps, shards, dirt and the tool in your free hand are 3D models on the limb. They move with it and hide behind your hands. In isometric view, and for the head, neck, chest and groin, the game is drawn on a flat limb at the centre of the screen.
description=[*] Your character takes off clothing that covers the wound first and puts it back on when you are done or when you stop. A sandbox option turns this off.
description=[*] Your hand shakes. Pain, panic, being drunk, exhausted, cold or tired make it worse. First Aid skill steadies it and makes the targets bigger.
description=[*] Clean work heals faster, stops bleeding and rarely infects.
description=[*] Sloppy work heals slower, can keep bleeding, often infects, and can leave glass or a bullet behind.
description=[*] Clean disinfecting can clear an infection.
description=[*] First Aid XP grows with quality.
description=[*] Botch it and the treatment fails: more pain, and the wound can start bleeding again.
description=[*] Right-click or Esc to stop at any time with no penalty.
description=[*] When the minigame ends, the treatment is done. No progress bar afterwards.
description=[*] Treating another player works too. You get the minigame, they get the result.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[b]Compatible with:[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Extensive Health Rework Evolved[/b]: replaces EHR's stitching minigame and passes your score to EHR, so EHR's own pain, infection and cellulitis rules still apply. Blood transfusions, drawing blood, injections, IV and emergency shots are needle minigames.
description=[*] [b]The Only Cure[/b]: amputation is a sawing minigame, cauterizing is holding the hot metal still, and cleaning the stump is scrubbing. With a hand gone, every minigame is harder and your hand shakes more. A prosthesis brings it back to normal.
description=[*] After an amputation, The Only Cure's own stitch and bandage are minigames as well.
description=[*] [b]Fancy Handwork / Brutal Handwork[/b]: works with off-hand items and their action changes.
description=[*] [b]The Only Cure x Extensive Health Rework Compatibility[/b]
description=[/list]
description=
description=[b]Sandbox options[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*] Turn the minigames on or off.
description=[*] Difficulty: Easy, Normal, Hard.
description=[*] Time limit for a minigame.
description=[*] Instant treatment on servers (off by default, risky: trusts each player's game).
description=[*] Whether quality and botching have effects.
description=[*] Undress the treated part (on by default).
description=[/list]
